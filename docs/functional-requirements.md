# PerfumeHub - Functional Requirements

## 1. Authentication

### Guest (Unauthenticated User)

- Register an account
- Log in to the system
- Browse the perfume catalog
- View perfume details

---

## 2. Perfume Catalog

### All users can:

#### Search perfumes

#### Filter perfumes by:
- Brand
- Notes
- Gender
- Perfume type
- Season
- Price

#### Sort perfumes by:
- Price
- Name
- Average rating

---

## 3. Price & Stock Tracking

### All users can:

- View product prices
- Check product availability

### Registered users can receive notifications when:

- The price of a perfume changes
- A perfume is back in stock

---

## 4. Reviews & Ratings

### Registered users can:

- Rate a perfume
- Write a review
- Edit their own review
- Delete their own review

---

## 5. Wishlist

### Registered users can:

- Add perfumes to their wishlist
- Remove perfumes from their wishlist
- View their wishlist

---

## 6. My Collection

### Registered users can:

- Add perfumes to their personal collection
- Remove perfumes from their personal collection
- Set the perfume status:
    - Owned
    - Previously Owned
    - Finished

---

## 7. Shopping

### Registered users can:

- Add products to the shopping cart
- Update product quantity
- Remove products from the shopping cart
- Place an order
- View order history

---

## 8. Administration

### Administrators can:

- Add new perfumes
- Edit perfume information
- Delete perfumes
- Manage product prices
- Manage stock inventory
- View users
- View customer orders
